## WHAT WOULD CHANGE THIS FORECAST

| If this turns out to be true | The roadmap changes like this |
|---|---|
| **Round 8 finds a signal that separates both mechanisms** | The trust gate becomes decidable, activation becomes a real Founder choice, and round 9 is scale. **This is the good path and it is one round away.** |
| **Round 8 finds no such signal** | **The honest product is a reader of real book text with honest gaps and page crops — not a tutor.** That is a legitimate product; it should be **chosen**, not arrived at. And `eligible for teaching` stays 0 indefinitely. |
| **The blind populations turn out to overlap the 97-row set** | The first real measurement is contaminated and a **third** population must be drawn — from a corpus that has now been looked at twice. |
| **The Founder switches on the sibling rule** | 31 mutilations disappear and **72 blocks leave a child's screen.** The first product change since round 6, and it is a subtraction. |
| **The Founder declines to merge, and says so as policy** | Integration branches stop being a delivery path by definition, and the composition-per-round cost becomes permanent and budgeted. |
| **The device stays unavailable another round** | **Round 8 would close with no device evidence for the second consecutive round**, and every UI fix since round 6 would be unverified where it counts. |
