## 1. THE PROPOSED NORTH STAR

> **MAKE THE TWO TEACHING-CRITICAL MECHANISMS VISIBLE TO A GATE.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **WHY NOW** | **Round 7 proved calibration cannot reach them**; the gate is built and waiting on evidence it cannot currently get. |
| **PROBLEM** | **Digit corruption survives character-exact two-stack agreement**; a **non-question served as a question** is a role error **no confidence score separates**. |
| **OBJECTIVE** | A signal — recognition-level or structural — that **separates each mechanism from correct content**. |
| **MEASURABLE TARGET** | Teaching-critical error bounded **below 0.0035 on the frozen blind population**, **or a measured statement that it cannot be**. |
| **DEPENDENCIES** | **The frozen populations exist. No Founder decision is needed to start.** |
| **RISKS** | **Building a signal that fits the 12 known errors.** |
| **MITIGATION** | **The blind populations are already frozen and hashed** — before anyone knew what round 8 would build. |
| **STOP CONDITION** | If no signal separates them, **report it and leave the gate unactivated — a truthful zero, again.** |
| **FOUNDER GATE** | Activation, still. **Unchanged.** |

**The mitigation is the strongest part of the proposal, and it is already banked.** Round 7 froze
two 120-lesson populations **and did not look at them**. A round-8 signal tuned on the 12 known
errors can be **tested against a population that predates it** — which is exactly what round 7
discovered the repository had never had before (*«the repository contains no pre-existing blind
population»*).

**The target's second clause matters as much as the first.** *«…or a measured statement that it
cannot be»* keeps round 8 honest in the same way round 7's stop condition did: **a negative result
is a deliverable, not a failure.**

## 4. WHAT ROUND 8 SHOULD NOT DO — on round 7's own evidence

- **Do not tune a signal on the 12 known errors and report it against them.** The frozen populations
  exist precisely so that cannot happen. **Use them once, and only once.**
- **Do not add the `option` type** — it converts a **loud** mutilation into a **quiet** one, and the
  option letters are still outside the only measurement the trust gate reads.
- **Do not add the `footnote` type** — 54 of 64 have no resolvable referent, ≥14 are not footnotes
  at all, and **the app has no rich text with which to render a footnote as a footnote.**
- **Do not add `ActivityKind.activity` before defect 6 closes** — 5 of the 50 are the publisher's
  colophon.
- **Do not quote «defect 6 is absent» without naming the surface.** Pack surface: 0 of 207. Lesson
  path: **22 served today.**
- **Do not treat any round-7 rate as transferable.** All of them are on **54 deliberately hard
  pages**.
- **Do not rsync `assets/fixtures/`** — it silently restores the crop-less artefact **whose lineage
  fields all read as current**. **Regenerate.**
- **Do not measure recognition recall without the population contract** — `DETECTED` **and**
  `APPLICABLE`, a scope predicate hashed **before** the draw, a control set in every draw. *A rate
  whose denominator includes the out-of-scope population is not a generalisation measure; it is a
  measure of how the detector was pointed.*
- **Do not let a fifth round compose on an unmerged fourth** without the Founder having stated a
  policy either way.
