## What is and is not the same

- **The documents and evidence are the same content**, from the same inputs.
- **The SHA-256 is different, and must be.** `15-FILE-MANIFEST.md` records the build time and the
  commit, so a rebuild is never byte-identical to its original. **Any citation of the original hash
  above will not match this file, and that is expected — it is not evidence of tampering.**
- **This regeneration therefore does not restore the original artefact's identity.** It restores its
  *contents*. A hash-addressed archive that has been rebuilt is a **new artefact carrying old
  information**, and this note exists so no later reader confuses the two.

## Why it was regenerated rather than reported and left

The build inputs live in a **session-scoped scratchpad that is cleaned when the session rolls**.
Waiting for a decision risked losing the ability to rebuild at all. **Regenerating is reversible —
this file can be deleted; not regenerating might not have been.**

**If the Founder would rather hold nothing than a re-derived copy, delete this ZIP.** The decision
is recorded here rather than assumed.
