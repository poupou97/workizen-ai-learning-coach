## Verdict — Layer A

Layer A is **RED for "trusted", YELLOW for "wired"**. The corpus is fully browsable (62,729 OCR pages, 3,240 lessons listed) but trust exists only where TC-v2 ran: 238 Science lessons, every one PARTIAL, at a measured 1-in-10 false-trust on hard pages that guards did not lower; everywhere else trust is 0 and what a child would read comes from the naive extractor TC-v1 measured at 1-in-3 wrong on hard pages, on lessons whose pages are 77 % hard. The 113 "proven" lessons are proven as wiring, not as truth: joined against TC-v2, 21 of the 32 overlapping lessons contain text TC-v2 withholds and 3 are attached to the wrong lesson; 2 of the 113 do not resolve to a canonical lesson; none has device evidence on disk. The packs on this Mac are the WAL-206 *variant* build, so 76 experiment-gated lessons would ship with any local APK. The role layer cannot ask (QUESTION 0.89, ACTIVITY 0.00), formulas are never trusted, and no statistical audit of served blocks exists. Autonomy over this layer should not be granted until G1–G6 exist as measured gates.
