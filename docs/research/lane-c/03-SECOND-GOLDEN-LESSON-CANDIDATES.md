## 2. What each strong candidate would need, and which assumptions it falsifies

### H1 — LS&ĐL 5 Bài 8 (recommended)

*Extraction (tc-v2-style):* Docling ▸ XY-cut on 4 pages; header attach (p38 «BÀI 8» inside a colour-heavy theme opener — the `page_feature:color_heavy` guard may withhold the objectives box, the Khởi động poem and KHÁM PHÁ question 1 on that page: the first risk to measure); `continues` for the Ngô Quyền story p40 → p41; `attribution` role for the four «(Theo …)» lines (gold p041 lists «attribution detached» as a critical class); `answer_slot` cells in the Luyện tập 1 table; the poem (Hồ Chí Minh, four lines, attributed) as a quoted block. No formula, no map, one diagram page.

*SemanticData:* **`TimelineSemantic`** — the type exists in `semantic_data.dart` with no derivation rule. Bài 8 offers the easiest possible test: the events are in *prose* on p39 as «Name (year – year)» (7 events, MEASURED years 179 TCN, 40–43, 248, 542–602, 713–722, 766–779, 905, 938) and the objective names the shape («vẽ được trục thời gian»). A deterministic rule `prose-dated-events-v1` (sibling of `tsl-summary-parenthesis-v1`) is falsifiable: **if it cannot recover ≥ 5 of 7 events with correct years from trusted blocks, the Timeline shape is not text-derivable even at its easiest** — which is a different question from the figure-timeline blocker in 05-VISUAL (LS&ĐL 8 p071 reversed by every parser). A second shape, **Source/Attribution** (`SourceClaim` in `source_reader.dart` already types it in the Deep path), is needed for the story boxes.

*Tutor loop / validator:* Luyện tập 1 (complete the timeline) is **deterministically checkable without SGV** — event ↔ year pairs come from the lesson's own trusted prose; a `TimelineValidator` is the History analogue of the Toán step validator. «Kể tên một số cuộc đấu tranh» is list membership (a set check, not a regex); «Kể lại câu chuyện» and «Chia sẻ» are participation-only (EvidencePolicy stays `none`). KHÁM PHÁ question 2 («quan sát các hình từ 1 đến 3») is figure-dependent → withheld → exactly the A19 class.

*Falsifies:* A01 (dash sub-items), A03 (no parenthesised summary), A05 (18/28 TOC gaps; 4 p by header), A06 («Chủ đề»), A07 (withheld reasons = colour + table-like, not diagram/math), A08 (attribution has no block kind), A09 (the timeline table needs cells), A10 (pairs/lists, not regex), A16 (History conventions), A17 (the gold itself), A19, A22 (no «Em đã học»). Leaves standing: A02 (no process), A14, A15, A20.

*Risks:* grade 5 (the persona «Na · Lớp 6» does not fit); colour-heavy p38 may withhold the whole opener; a short attributed poem (D4 internal); two SGV reference stories the teacher-text guard must keep out.

## 3. Recommendation

**Propose LS&ĐL 5 Bài 8 «Đấu tranh giành độc lập thời kì Bắc thuộc» as the second golden lesson.** It is the strongest falsifier with sufficient evidence: it attacks 12 of the 22 ledger assumptions (the whole semantic and tutor layer plus every KNTT-Science furniture string), it does so on the modality the Founder named (timeline + source reasoning), and it is the only non-Science candidate that already carries a measured trust number inside its pages (gold p041: TLSR 1.0, FTR 0, 3/3 questions) plus a found printed header and an SGV lesson entry with section-context keys. Its timeline lives in prose and in a completion table — so it tests whether `TimelineSemantic` can ever be *text-derived*, a question the figure-timeline blocker cannot answer. If it passes, the model gains a second semantic shape, a second block kind (source + attribution) and a second deterministic validator that is not math. If it fails, it fails on named rules (`prose-dated-events-v1`, `attribution` role, colour-heavy guard) — not on taste.

Toán 6 Bài 9 is the recommended *third* slice (same persona, RULE/EXAMPLE/EXERCISE + typed validator), once a Toán role gold of ≥ 30 questions exists.
