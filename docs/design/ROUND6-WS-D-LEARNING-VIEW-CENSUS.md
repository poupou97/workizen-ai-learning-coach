# WS-D reading of the census — FORMS BEFORE RULES

Everything above is machine-generated by `tool/evidence/learning_view_census.py` over
the 238 canonical TSLs (`tc-v2/tc2-p1`, six Khoa học / KHTN books, grades 4–9). Nothing
below adds a number; it only says what the numbers mean and what they forbid.

**Denominator, stated once: 238 lessons bridged, 0 refused.** Every rate here is out of
238. This is the *Science* corpus only — there is no Toán, Tiếng Việt, LS&ĐL or Tin học
TSL in the canonical store at all, so nothing here generalises to those subjects.

## 1. The number the product turns on

**✨ Trực quan has something to show for 73 of 238 lessons — 0.307.**

For the other **165 lessons (0.693)** a child opening Trực quan reads
«Chưa có sơ đồ cho bài này — chỉ có bảng tóm tắt lời sách». That is honest, and it is
also the ceiling on every visual-grammar investment measured against real data rather
than against gold pages.

**🦉 Học với SAM exists for 1 of 238 lessons — 0.004.** One hand-written script
(KHTN 6 Bài 17). SAM's teaching reach on the canonical corpus is **one lesson**, and the
Learning View census is where that stops being a slogan and becomes a number.

## 2. Two renderer families have NO real data behind them

| kind | instances in 238 lessons | rule | renderer exists? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `process` | **181** | `tsl-enumerated-steps-v1` | yes — `ProcessFlowView` |
| `comparison` | **6** | `tsl-summary-parenthesis-v1` | yes — `MindmapView` |
| `conceptMap` | **0** | — | yes — `MindmapView` |
| `timeline` | **0** | — | yes — `TimelineView` |

`process` carries **96.8 %** of everything the visual layer has ever been handed.
`comparison` is **6 instances across 238 lessons — 0.025 per lesson**, the same
nearly-empty shape round 5 measured for date/TIMELINE (3 of 12, coverage 0.140, firing
on exactly 3 of 224 pages). `conceptMap` and `timeline` are **empty on this corpus**:
`TimelineView` is exercised only by LS&ĐL, which is not in the canonical store, and by
the synthetic fixture.

**This is the argument against expanding renderer families, stated in the corpus's own
numbers.** Four families exist; one has mass, one is marginal, two are empty. Adding a
fifth before a form census would repeat exactly the round-5 result the Founder told us
not to repeat.

## 3. Where the mass actually is — and it is not a new renderer

**2 170 blocks are withheld (0.152 of 14 241 learning blocks).** The composition
decides what would repay work:

| reason | blocks | share of withheld | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `agree_text` | 824 | **0.380** | the two OCR stacks disagree — WS-B's territory |
| `figure_dependent` | 632 | **0.291** | the text cannot stand without a figure |
| `agree_order` | 224 | 0.103 | reading-order disagreement |
| `page_feature:diagram` | 197 | 0.091 | the page is diagram-heavy |
| `page_feature:color_heavy` | 95 | 0.044 | |

| `answer_leak` | 29 | 0.013 | correctly withheld, never to be recovered |
| others | 46 | 0.021 | `table_without_cells` 20 · `low_ocr_conf` 19 · `role_conflict` 7 |

Two rows are WS-D's to act on and they point in different directions:

**(a) `figure_dependent` 632 blocks = 0.291 of all withholding, against 3 864 figures in
the corpus.** This is census-backed support for E1's nomination of **LABELED_FIGURE** —
it is the single largest *presentational* reason a child cannot read something. It is
NOT support for building it yet: knowing where the mass is is step 2 of
`FORM CENSUS → CLUSTER → REPRESENTATIVE EXAMPLES → RULE → HOLDOUT → MEASURE →
GENERALIZE`, and the cluster and representative-example steps have not been done.

**(b) `unknown_role:*` — 118 blocks withheld because the CONSUMER has no type, not
because the text is untrusted.** `footnote` 64 · `activity` 50 · `option` 4. These blocks
passed every trust gate and are withheld anyway. That is a **model gap, not a data gap**,
and it is the cheapest measured win on the board — no recognition work, no new rule, no
renderer family: three block types in `lib/core/lesson_model/**` (workstream C's file).

The `option` count is 4 and looks negligible, but it is the shape round 5's 97-row audit
named as defect #8: **an incomplete multiple-choice answer set, where withholding is not
the safe move.** Small count, sharp edge.

## 4. What this census does NOT say

- Nothing about **Toán · Tiếng Việt · LS&ĐL · Tin học** — no canonical TSL exists for
  them. Round 5 already measured that E2's sequence rule produced *nothing at all* on
  those three subjects; this census cannot even attempt them.
- Nothing about **image forms**. The bridge ran without crops, so `image` blocks are
  absent from the block-type histogram; the 3 864 figures are counted from the TSLs and
  are a **different measurement**, deliberately not merged into that table.
- Nothing about **quality**. A lesson that yields a `process` is counted here whether the
  flow is right or wrong. `HAS A DIAGRAM != THE DIAGRAM IS CORRECT`.
- Nothing about **learning**. `OPENED != UNDERSTOOD` · `READ != MASTERY` ·
  `TAP != COMPETENCE`.

## 5. Recommendation — bounded, and it is not «build a renderer»

1. **Do not add a renderer family this round.** Four exist; two have zero real data.
2. **The next measured step is the `unknown_role:*` 118**, and it belongs to workstream C
   (`lib/core/lesson_model/**`): three block types would un-withhold blocks that already
   passed every trust gate.
3. **LABELED_FIGURE is the right *candidate* for a form census next round**, on the
   strength of `figure_dependent` = 0.291 — cluster and representative examples first,
   rule last.
4. **`comparison` should be watched, not extended.** 6 instances across 238 lessons is
   not yet evidence that `tsl-summary-parenthesis-v1` generalises.
